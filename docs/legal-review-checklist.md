# Legal and Privacy Review Checklist

**Status:** Draft checklist — not legal advice and not a completed legal review  
**Release gate:** Required before public launch

## Website identity and ownership

- [ ] Confirm the final public brand name and owner/operator information.
- [ ] Complete preliminary trademark and common-law searches for the final name, logo, tagline, mascot, and related product names.
- [ ] Review copyright, trademark, and licensing risks for every public-facing asset.
- [ ] Obtain owner approval for the final brand identity and production artwork.
- [ ] Add a working contact method and confirm who is responsible for site notices and corrections.

## Educational and automotive disclaimers

- [ ] Have qualified counsel review the educational-use disclaimer and any warranty/liability language.
- [ ] Confirm that the site clearly distinguishes symptoms, possible causes, diagnostic tests, and confirmed faults.
- [ ] Confirm that diagnostic trouble codes are never presented as proof that a component has failed.
- [ ] Confirm that vehicle-specific procedures and specifications defer to the correct manufacturer service information.
- [ ] Review stop-work warnings for lifting, hot components, electrical systems, batteries, fuel, brakes, steering, and other safety-critical systems.
- [ ] Confirm that content review and correction procedures are documented.

## Privacy and data handling

- [ ] Verify the actual production hosting provider and its privacy/data-processing terms.
- [ ] Confirm whether the host processes access logs, IP addresses, cookies, or other request metadata and reflect this accurately in the privacy notice.
- [ ] Recheck the application for analytics, advertising, telemetry, embedded third-party scripts, and external data transfers before release.
- [ ] Confirm that the Case Worksheet remains local-only or revise the notice if persistence or transmission is later added.
- [ ] If accounts, saved histories, uploads, or customer records are introduced, define collection purpose, access controls, retention, deletion, and security requirements before implementation.
- [ ] Review third-party link disclosures and any external service terms.

## Accessibility and consumer notices

- [ ] Review accessibility requirements applicable to the operating business and audience.
- [ ] Confirm that safety warnings, content status, and interactive controls are accessible to keyboard and assistive-technology users.
- [ ] Confirm that the site does not imply professional certification, manufacturer affiliation, or endorsement where none exists.
- [ ] Confirm that any future pricing, subscriptions, trials, cancellation terms, or payment disclosures are separately reviewed before monetization.
