# Project Decision Log

## 2026-10-30 — Public experience and identity development

**Decision:** Continue building the public-facing educational experience and original identity before beginning one comprehensive testing and remediation phase.

**Implementation approach:**
- Continue feature development in isolated GitHub branches.
- Avoid repeatedly pausing for full test cycles while features are unfinished.
- Preserve normal development discipline; do not knowingly release broken or unsafe code.
- Perform one consolidated browser, responsive, accessibility, automotive-content, security, and end-to-end remediation phase once the public foundation is assembled.

**Brand constraints:**
- The Diagnostic Bay remains the project label, not an approved commercial brand.
- Burning Oil Garage, Grease Dojo, and other explored names remain concepts only.
- The mascot and helper remain unnamed.
- The new emblem and mechanic illustration are original concept assets, not final approved artwork.
- Anime-horror inspiration is tonal only; no recognizable franchise character, costume, logo, weapon, or promotional composition may be reproduced.

**Content constraints:**
- The three supplied content rounds are the source basis for the current draft library.
- All code pages and field guides remain unverified drafts pending mechanic review and authoritative references.
- No vehicle-specific specifications or torque values are invented.
- The safety lesson has only 15 supplied quiz questions; the planned 20-question lesson is not represented as complete.

**Release and infrastructure constraints:**
- No deployment, domain purchase, paid service, production database change, or production release is authorized by this development work.
- Supabase security findings remain separately tracked. This branch does not attempt database remediation or introduce a frontend Supabase connection.
