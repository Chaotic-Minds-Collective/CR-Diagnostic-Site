## Required before final brand approval

- Create and review original production mascot and logo artwork.
- Audit all names, taglines, copy, and visual assets for trademark, copyright, and licensing risk.
- Conduct preliminary trademark and common-law searches; document unresolved concerns.
- Obtain explicit owner approval before replacing provisional public identity assets.
- Keep production-ready source files and export variants organized separately from concept studies.

No external franchise character or franchise logo is approved for use in public-facing commercial branding.
