THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

Some problems
aren't DIY.

Confidence is useful. Knowing when to stop is a diagnostic skill, too.

⚠

Never trust a jack alone

Work on level, stable ground with correctly rated jack stands at approved lift points. If you cannot support the vehicle safely, stop.

ϟ

Respect electricity and fuel

Short circuits can cause burns, fire, or module damage. Keep sparks and flames away from fuel and follow vehicle-specific procedures.

◉

Hot parts stay hot

Let the engine and exhaust cool. Never open a hot pressurized cooling system. Never run an engine in an enclosed space.

↗

Use the right service data

Pinouts, torque values, thresholds, and procedures vary by vehicle. This site will not invent specifications to fill a gap.
